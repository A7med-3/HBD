# How to use this birthday website (no coding)

Everything personal is in **`js/config.js`** — open it in Notepad and change her name, your name, and the messages.

Photos go in the **`images`** folder (see `PUT-YOUR-PHOTOS-HERE.txt`).

---

## 1. Preview on your computer

1. Open the folder `birthday-site`.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** — it opens in your browser.
3. Click through the pages like she will.

---

## 2. Put it online (so email link works)

She needs a **link** like `https://something.netlify.app` — not a file on your PC.

### Easiest: Netlify Drop (free, ~2 minutes)

1. Go to [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) in Chrome or Edge.
2. **Drag the whole `birthday-site` folder** onto the page (must include `index.html`, `css`, `js`, `images`).
3. Wait until Netlify shows a URL (random name like `joyful-cupcake-abc123.netlify.app`).
4. Click the link to test on your phone.
5. Optional: in Netlify site settings you can rename the site to something cuter.

Copy that URL and paste it in your email to her.

### Alternative: GitHub Pages

If you already use GitHub, push the folder and enable Pages — ask a friend or follow GitHub’s “Pages” docs. Netlify Drop is simpler for a one-time gift.

---

## 3. Email example
